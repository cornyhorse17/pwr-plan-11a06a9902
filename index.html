220 kV Power Receiving Plan

Early Power, Block 1. Option B (through the 220 kV switchyard and autotransformer) shown beside the unchanged 500 kV baseline. Working plan of 10 November 2026. All dates provisional.

Summary and decision ask

The baseline stays as it is: power through the 500 kV switchyard and first synchronisation of GT11 on 22 December 2026. Option B is a prepared alternative for receiving grid power on 1 December 2026 if the 500 kV grid connection is not available in time.

Power receiving through 220 kV
Feasible with conditions

The plant design and the power receiving procedure already foresee this route. The date depends on four conditions listed below, none of which is evidenced today.

GT11 export to 220 kV in reverse
Feasible with conditions

The autotransformer bank is rated well above one gas turbine. The governing condition is the System Operator's approval of a staged connection scheme and its protection settings.

Plan result at current assumptions
–

Conditions that decide 1 December

  • A 220 kV line to the plant is physically connected and KEGOC allows it to be made live under a staged scheme.
  • Signed completion and site test records exist for the 220 kV switchyard, the autotransformer and their protection and control cabinets. The records on file today cover the 500 kV panels only.
  • Protection settings for the staged network are calculated and agreed with KEGOC. The settings on file are design values for the final network.
  • Commercial metering is accepted and an import arrangement exists for the energy taken.

What we ask from the head office

  • Agree by 12 November that Option B is prepared as a contingency, with no change to the 500 kV baseline.
  • Raise with the consortium leader the early release of 220 kV and autotransformer commissioning resources, which its schedule shows in 2027.
  • Support one joint request to the Employer and, through it, to KEGOC for a staged connection through 220 kV.
  • Accept that this is a plan to receive power. It is not a statement of readiness; that follows only from an evidence-based gate.
